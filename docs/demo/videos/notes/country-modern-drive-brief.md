# Modern country production brief — driving Nashville / Wallen-Combs class

Genre 3 of 4. Original instrumental only. Style-of modern country (Morgan Wallen / Luke Combs / Post Malone-country class: acoustic foundation, Tele lead, punchy backbeat). Do not quote any copyrighted melody or lyric.

Sources (accessed 2026-08-31):

- BeatKey, "How to Make Country Music": modern/bro-country 80-110 BPM in G/D/A; country pop 100-130; I-V-vi-IV (G-D-Em-C) is the pop-country loop; instruments = acoustic guitar backbone, Telecaster chicken-pick lead, clean electric bass, acoustic kit with kick 1+3 / snare 2+4 / 8th hats. Avoid synth bass and 808s.
- Make Pop Music, "How To Make A Country Song (Post Malone, Luke Combs, Bailey Zimmerman, Kane Brown, HARDY)" (2024-07-19): verse acoustic + drums + bass + melodic guitar slides; pre-chorus lifts with electric; chorus stacks acoustic + electric + slide; drums stay simple and punchy.
- Hits Songs Deconstructed 2024 #1-hit notes: country #1s used acoustic + electric guitar, acoustic drums, bass; intro often an instrumental hook.

## Instrumentation (MusicXML ensemble, not piano)

| Part                   | MusicXML / GM                                            | Role                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Acoustic Guitar        | GM 26 steel-string                                       | Strummed backbone. Quarters on the verse, fuller halves on the chorus. |
| Overdriven Guitar      | GM 30 (clean-enough Tele-class lead)                     | Short slide-like 8th figures and a chorus hook. Not a metal chug.      |
| Electric Bass (finger) | GM 34                                                    | Root on 1, fifth on 3. Walking a step into each new bar on the chorus. |
| Drum Kit               | unpitched GM kit ch.10: kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42 | Kick 1+3, snare 2+4, hats 8ths. No four-on-the-floor, no 808.          |

## Tempo, key, length

- 108 BPM, 4/4, G major (one sharp). Guitar-open key (BeatKey); sitting in the modern country-pop window, not a ballad and not bluegrass.
- 24 bars = 53.33s written (`24 * 4 * 60 / 108`). Under the 60s render cap. Acoustic guitar is not a synth pad, so QC tail is the 3s base.
- Divisions=2 (8th-note grid).

## Harmony and groove

Loop: G | D | Em | C (I - V - vi - IV). Regular 8-bar phrases (two times through the loop).

Bass: G2 / D2 / E2 / C2 with a fifth on beat 3.

Kick 1+3, snare 2+4, hats 8ths. Straight grid (modern country pop, not honky-tonk shuffle).

## Arrangement arc (24 bars)

1. Bars 1-4: intro hook. Acoustic + lead figure, hats only (no kick/snare).
2. Bars 5-12: verse. Acoustic quarters, bass, full backbeat; lead tacet after a one-bar answer.
3. Bars 13-16: pre-chorus lift. Acoustic denser, lead 8ths climb.
4. Bars 17-22: chorus. Open acoustic halves, bass walking, lead hook, full kit.
5. Bars 23-24: tag on G. Final snare fill into a held G.

## Anti-patterns

- Piano-led writing or synth pads substituting for acoustic/Tele.
- 808 / trap hats (that is country-trap, not this brief).
- Copying Last Night / Beautiful Crazy / I Got Better / A Bar Song riffs, or any lyric.
- Scores much shorter than ~40s.
- Pitched "drum" notes instead of unpitched kit.
