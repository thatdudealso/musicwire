# Musicwire showcase production briefs

These briefs are the compositional contract for the nine captain-certified public gallery scores.
Each score is written as a complete 45-60 second miniature with an intro, development, and close.
The generated source of truth is `scripts/generate-showcase-examples.mjs`; the verbatim public
MusicXML files live in `static/examples/`.

| #   | Track                | Genre and mood                                     | Tempo and key        | Instrumentation                               | Arrangement arc                                                                                                                                                                    | Anti-patterns avoided                                                                                                 |
| --- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01  | Moonlit Thread       | Restless chamber lyric, intimate rather than showy | 84 BPM, D minor      | Solo violin                                   | A rising four-note cell opens quietly, moves into a higher sequential answer, then returns to a hushed D                                                                           | No scale exercise, no perpetual forte, no impossible double stops                                                     |
| 02  | Sunroom Parade       | Bright, happy salon miniature                      | 96 BPM, C major      | Solo piano                                    | A buoyant I-IV-V idea establishes the room, a higher middle strain adds lift, then the opening cadence returns with a soft smile                                                   | No generic unshaped noodling, no muddy bass cluster, no static chord loop                                             |
| 03  | Cedar Lantern        | Warm, reflective fingerstyle                       | 84 BPM, E minor      | Solo classical guitar                         | Open-string arpeggio introduction, a wider-register answer over a descending harmonic path, then a ringing E-minor landing                                                         | No block-piano writing, no dense chords, no abrupt ending                                                             |
| 04  | Daybreak Kite        | Airy and playful                                   | 88 BPM, D major      | Solo flute                                    | A breath before an ascending call, a nimble upper-register development, then a gentle descending landing                                                                           | No breathless continuous high notes, no scale-only phrase, no low unidiomatic flute writing                           |
| 05  | Echoes Without Words | Still, wordless choral meditation                  | 80 BPM, A minor      | Synthesized choir voice, Ahh sound            | Two-measure legato cells establish the melody, a single crest widens the range, then it fades on A                                                                                 | No lyrics or claim of human singing, no clipped syllabic rhythm, no choir masquerading as a solo vocalist             |
| 06  | Winter Window        | Intimate, slightly wintry chamber music            | 76 BPM, G minor      | String quartet: two violins, viola, cello     | Violin I states a restrained motif over a close four-part texture, the middle opens the register, and the quartet settles back to G                                                | No unison-only ensemble, no identical rhythmic duplication across all parts, no non-idiomatic string extremes         |
| 07  | Bright Map           | Buoyant neoclassical travel sketch                 | 84 BPM, B-flat major | Flute, B-flat clarinet, bassoon, piano        | Flute introduces the melodic route, winds trade harmonic support above bassoon and piano, then a compact tutti cadence resolves it                                                 | No piano-only piece with incidental winds, no constant tutti doubling, no bassoon treated as a high melody instrument |
| 08  | Lantern Call         | Dark, uplifting house/EDM with a clear drop        | 126 BPM, C minor     | Synth lead, synth bass, synth pad, house kit  | Pads establish the intro, a four-on-the-floor groove arrives, the lead climbs through an eight-bar build, a two-bar break removes the floor, and the full hook returns at the drop | No straight-through loop, no acoustic rhythm-section substitute, no drop without a preceding reduction in energy      |
| 09  | After Hours Signal   | Relaxed easy-swing club closer                     | 104 BPM, F major     | Alto saxophone, piano, acoustic bass, drumset | Sax states a compact head, piano and walking bass sustain the changes with a steady drum pulse, then the head cools into a final F                                                 | No fake swing made of straight scales, no bass that merely doubles piano roots, no drumset rendered as pitched melody |

The tracks are deliberately short-form compositions, not prompts for generated style imitation.
They favor clear motifs, playable registers, and a definite ending so the browser audio has a
reason to be replayed.
