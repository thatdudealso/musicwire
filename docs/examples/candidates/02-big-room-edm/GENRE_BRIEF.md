# Big-room EDM production brief

Internal lane: festival builds, drops, supersaw-style leads, four-on-the-floor (Martin Garrix energy class). Original instrumental only. Do not quote any copyrighted melody, riff, or lyric.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- The Ghost Production, "EDM festival tracks: 9 Stage Rules" (2026-07-10): big-room/mainstage tempo 126-130 BPM (128 DJ-compatible); skeleton is kick, bass, lead hook, clap/snare; keys F/G/A minor sit well on large systems.
- Melodigging, "Big Room House": 126-132 BPM; four-on-the-floor; supersaw or horn-like stabs; lush reverberant breakdown vs dry punchy drop; intro 8-16 bars, breakdown, 8-16 bar build (snare rolls), 16-32 bar drop.
- Red Star Media, "Structuring Your EDM Tracks": classic energy curve intro 3/10, build 6/10, drop 9/10, breakdown 4/10, second drop 10/10.

This candidate is longer than the existing 24-bar festival demo (`Skyline Surge` in F minor). New key, new hook, full two-drop arc.

## Tempo, key, length

- 128 BPM, 4/4, G minor (two flats). Festival-safe per Ghost Production; distinct from the F-minor demo.
- 88 bars = 165.0s written.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: Gm | Eb | Bb | F (i - VI - III - VII). Regular 8-bar phrases.

Kick every quarter. Closed hat every eighth. Snare on 2 and 4. Last build bar is a 16th snare roll.

## Arrangement arc (88 bars)

1. Bars 1-16: pad intro, then groove (kick/hat/snare + bass) from bar 9. Lead tacet.
2. Bars 17-32: build. Lead rising cell, then climb. Last bar snare roll.
3. Bars 33-40: break. Drums and bass out; pad piano; held lead.
4. Bars 41-56: drop 1. Full kit, bass, pad forte, saw hook in eighths.
5. Bars 57-64: second break, thinner than the first.
6. Bars 65-80: drop 2 (peak). Hook an octave higher.
7. Bars 81-88: outro. Strip to kick + pad, cadence on G.

## Anti-patterns

- Piano-led writing.
- Straight loop with no break before the drop.
- Acoustic drumset substituting for the house kit.
- Copying festival catalog hooks (including the F-minor demo).
- Pitched drum notes.
