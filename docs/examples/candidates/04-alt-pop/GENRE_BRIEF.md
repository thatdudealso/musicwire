# Alt-pop production brief

Internal lane: genre-blending piano/synth + rhythmic drive, moody verse to explosive chorus (Twenty One Pilots energy class). Original instrumental only.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- Open Music Theory, "Form in pop/rock music": verse-chorus with prechorus as energy slingshot; choruses thicker and more regular than verses.
- BeatKey, "How to make pop music": verses often kick 1+3; choruses may jump to four-on-the-floor; prechorus pulls back so the chorus launches.
- MTO 30.2 Geary, "Formal Functions of Drum Patterns": in verse-prechorus-chorus, drums mark initiation, buildup, and arrival; pattern change is the form.
- Lyric Assistant pop-rock notes on dynamics: pocket verse, push chorus.

The lane is not piano-solo. Piano is the verse identity; drums, bass, and synth must explode the chorus.

## Instrumentation

| ---------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Piano      | GM 1 acoustic grand                 | Moody verse ostinato and chorus doubling.                  |
| Synth Lead | GM 81 square                        | Chorus hook only. Tacet on verses.                         |
| Synth Bass | GM 39                               | Sparse whole notes in verse; driving eighths in chorus.    |
| Drum Kit   | kick 36, snare 38, hat 42, crash 49 | Verse: snare + hat, almost no kick. Chorus: full backbeat. |

## Tempo, key, length

- 96 BPM, 4/4, C minor (three flats). Midtempo so the chorus double-time _feel_ comes from drum density, not a tempo map.
- 72 bars = 180.0s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Verse loop: Cm | Ab | Eb | Gm (i - VI - III - v).
Chorus loop: Cm | Bb | Ab | G (i - VII - VI - V) - brighter motion into G.

Verse drums: hats eighths, snare on 4 only (uneasy). Chorus: kick 1+3, snare 2+4, crash on 1.

## Arrangement arc (72 bars)

1. Bars 1-8: piano intro, no drums.
2. Bars 9-24: verse. Piano ostinato, ghost bass, snare-on-4.
3. Bars 25-32: prechorus. Kick enters on 1; piano climbs.
4. Bars 33-48: chorus. Full kit, synth hook, bass eighths.
5. Bars 49-56: verse two (stripped).
6. Bars 57-72: final chorus + tag on C.

## Anti-patterns

- Piano miniature with decorative drums.
- Same drum pattern in verse and chorus.
- Copying any named-lane catalog melody.
- Four-on-the-floor from bar 1 (destroys the explosion).
