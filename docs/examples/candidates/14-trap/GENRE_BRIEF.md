# Trap instrumental production brief

**Why this lane:** Hip-hop/trap instrumentals dominate current streaming beat culture and are absent from the live showcase (which is chamber + one house cut). A half-time 140 BPM 808 + rolling-hat lane is the cleanest popular contrast to four-on-the-floor and to R&B's slower pocket.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- SONIQ, "How to Write a Trap Song": 130-145 BPM with half-time feel (~70 perceived); 808 bass carries low-end melody; triplet/16th hat rolls; dark minor; snare often on beat 3 in the half-time grid.
- Sample Focus tempo guide: trap 130-145, usually produced at 130-140 so hats can be detailed; kick/808 on 1 and 3 of the slow feel.
- Beat Kitchen: trap does not use a fixed four-on-the-floor kick; 808 is a pitched bass instrument; hats are the signature (16th/32nd rolls). MusicXML will use 16ths (divisions=4), not 32nds.

## Instrumentation

| Part       | MusicXML / GM             | Role                                                |
| ---------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Synth Bass | GM 39                     | Pitched 808-class bass (roots + sliding fifths).    |
| Synth Pad  | GM 89                     | Dark minor bed.                                     |
| Synth Lead | GM 82                     | Sparse bell-like hook. Not a pop chorus singer.     |
| Trap Kit   | kick 36, snare 38, hat 42 | Half-time: snare on beat 3; 16th hats; sparse kick. |

## Tempo, key, length

- 140 BPM, 4/4, E minor (one sharp). Standard trap grid, half-time snare.
- 96 bars = 164.6s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: Em | C | G | D (i - VI - III - VII). 808 plays the roots as long notes with a fifth grace at bar ends.

Kit: kick on 1, snare on 3, 16th hats throughout the drop. Verse hats on eighths only.

## Arrangement arc (96 bars)

1. Bars 1-16: pad + 808, no hats.
2. Bars 17-32: verse. Eighth hats, snare on 3.
3. Bars 33-48: hook. 16th hats, lead enters.
4. Bars 49-56: break (silence in hats).
5. Bars 57-80: drop two / peak hat density.
6. Bars 81-96: outro, 808 cadence on E.

## Anti-patterns

- Four-on-the-floor.
- Live acoustic country kit (lane 10).
- R&B Rhodes as the identity (lane 11).
- Copying any named-trap catalog 808 melody.
- Pitched drum notes instead of unpitched hats.
