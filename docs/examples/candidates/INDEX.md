## Extra lanes (11-14)

Chosen to round out a popular-genre showcase without duplicating four-on-the-floor club music or the existing chamber/jazz/house miniatures:

- **Contemporary R&B** - chart-current pocket/Rhodes lane the live gallery lacks.
- **Synthwave** - analog-retro electronic, distinct from house/techno/big-room.
- **Drum and bass** - UK breakbeat + Reese bass; the only non-four-on-the-floor dance lane.
- **Trap instrumental** - half-time 808 + rolling hats; the hip-hop beat lane.

## Hard rules honored

- Original composition, instrumental only.
- 2.5-3:00 written duration at notated tempo.
- Genre-correct GM / unpitched kit mapping.
- Local validation only (no paid `/v1/validate`, no hosted MCP, no MP3 render).
- Live `static/examples/` and `catalog.json` untouched.
