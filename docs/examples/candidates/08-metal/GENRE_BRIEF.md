# Metal production brief

Riff-led heavy track. Distinct from metalcore (lane 09) and from the existing nu-metal SOD-AI style. Original instrumental only.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- Guitar World, "How to gallop on guitar" (2025-03-04): the gallop (eighth + two 16ths per beat) is the core traditional-metal rhythm; counted 1-&-a; not a triplet; associated with classic heavy/thrash vocabularies.
- Wikipedia, "Heavy metal gallop": eighth then two 16ths, usually on palm-muted power chords; drums often double-kick the same skeleton.
- Orphiq, "Types of Metal Music": traditional/heavy metal 100-150 BPM, galloping riffs, twin guitar harmonies in diatonic thirds; metalcore 120-160 with breakdowns and scream/clean contrast. This lane is the former.
- Guitar Chalk, metal rhythm patterns (2025-11-26): palm muting, downstroke precision, gallops; syncopated chugs belong to modern/metalcore, not this brief.

**Not metalcore:** no half-time breakdown, no silence-before-drop, no one-chug-pattern verse. **Not nu-metal:** no bounced hip-hop backbeat, no 7-string djent syncopation.

## Tempo, key, length

- 160 BPM, 4/4, E minor (one sharp). Traditional/thrash overlap without copying any catalog riff.
- 112 bars = 168.0s.
- Divisions=4 (required for the gallop).

## Harmony and groove

Riff cells on Em | G | D | C (i - III - VII - VI) power chords. Twin guitar plays diatonic thirds above the open chorus figures only.

Gallop: E2 eighth + two 16ths, four times per bar, palm-muted on verses; open power-chord halves on the refrain.

## Arrangement arc (112 bars)

1. Bars 1-16: intro gallop, kick+hats, no harmony guitar.
2. Bars 17-48: verse riffs (two 16-bar statements).
3. Bars 49-64: refrain. Open chords, twin thirds.
4. Bars 65-80: verse three (new register on the riff, still gallop).
5. Bars 81-96: refrain two.
6. Bars 97-112: outro gallop cadence on E. No breakdown.

## Anti-patterns

- Half-time chug breakdown (lane 09).
- Nu-metal bounce / rap-adjacent backbeat.
- Clean piano or synth carrying the riff.
- Copying any Maiden/Priest/Metallica gallop melody.
- Pitched drum notes.
