# Contemporary R&B production brief

**Why this lane:** R&B is a current-chart staple missing from the existing acoustic/EDM/metal/country demo set. It rounds the gallery toward late-night groove, Rhodes, and pocket drums rather than festival or guitar anthems. Internal artist-class references (Kehlani / Brent Faiyaz / Summer Walker energy) stay in this brief.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- SONIQ, "How to Write an R&B Song": contemporary R&B 60-100 BPM, often 90-100; intro 4-8, verse 8-16, prechorus, chorus; kick/snare locked to bass.
- Hot Mic Radio, R&B subgenre guide (2026-07-14): contemporary R&B 85-105 BPM; sequenced hip-hop-influenced drums; synths, 808s, live guitar; kick on 1 (occasionally 3), snare/clap on 2 and 4.
- Monosounds, "How to Make RnB Music in 2026": alt-R&B / neo-soul 85-105; drums should feel played even when programmed; ghost notes and velocity conversation between kick and bass.
- Make Pop Music, "How To Make Modern R&B": Rhodes/keys, swelling bass, staccato keys, chorus drums denser than verse.

## Instrumentation

| Electric Piano | GM 5 Rhodes                        | Warm voicings. Verse identity.                                       |

| Synth Bass     | GM 39 (808-class sub)              | Swelling roots; ghost fifths.                                        |
| Drum Kit       | kick 36, snare 38, hat 42, open 46 | Sparse verse (kick 1, snare 3); chorus adds 2+4 snare and 16th hats. |

## Tempo, key, length

- 86 BPM, 4/4, B-flat minor (five flats). Contemporary pocket, not trap-soul 70-with-double-time.
- 64 bars = 178.6s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: Bbm | Gb | Ab | Db (i - VI - VII - III). Jazzier than pop I-V-vi-IV without quoting neo-soul catalog.

Verse drums: kick on 1, snare on 3 (half-time pocket), hats on offbeats.
Chorus drums: snare 2+4, extra kick on 3, 16th closed hats.

## Arrangement arc (64 bars)

1. Bars 1-8: Rhodes intro, no kick.
2. Bars 9-24: verse.
3. Bars 25-32: prechorus (hats denser).
4. Bars 33-48: chorus.
5. Bars 49-56: verse two.
6. Bars 57-64: chorus tag on Bb.

## Anti-patterns

- Four-on-the-floor house.
- Metal distortion.
- Piano-only quiet-storm with no kit contrast.
- Copying named-class toplines.
- Trap 32nd hat rolls as the identity (that's lane 14).
