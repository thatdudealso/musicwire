# Modern country production brief

Internal lane: twangy guitars, steady backbeat, warm melodic hooks (Morgan Wallen energy class). Original instrumental only. Longer than the existing 24-bar demo (`Cedar Mile` in G).

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- BeatKey, "How to Make Country Music": modern/bro-country 80-110 BPM in G/D/A; country-pop 100-130; acoustic guitar backbone, Telecaster chicken-pick lead, clean electric bass, acoustic kit with kick 1+3 / snare 2+4 / 8th hats. Avoid synth bass and 808s.
- Make Pop Music, "How To Make A Country Song" (2024-07-19): verse acoustic + drums + bass + guitar slides; pre-chorus lifts with electric; chorus stacks acoustic + electric; drums stay simple and punchy.
- Hits Songs Deconstructed 2024 notes (via the existing country demo brief): country #1s used acoustic + electric guitar, acoustic drums, bass; intro often an instrumental hook.

## Tempo, key, length

- 96 BPM, 4/4, A major (three sharps). Guitar-open key; distinct from the G-major 108 BPM demo.
- 68 bars = 170.0s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: A | E | F#m | D (I - V - vi - IV). Regular 8-bar phrases.

Straight grid, not shuffle, not trap-country.

## Arrangement arc (68 bars)

1. Bars 1-8: intro hook (acoustic + Tele, hats only).
2. Bars 9-24: verse.
3. Bars 25-32: prechorus lift.
4. Bars 33-48: chorus.
5. Bars 49-56: verse two.
6. Bars 57-68: chorus + tag on A.

## Anti-patterns

- 808 / trap hats (lane 14).
- Piano-led writing.
- Four-on-the-floor.
- Copying the G-major `Cedar Mile` hook.
- Synth bass.
