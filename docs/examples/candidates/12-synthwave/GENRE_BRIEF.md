# Synthwave production brief

**Why this lane:** Synthwave is a widely recognized modern-retro electronic style that showcases analog-drum + saw-lead mapping in MuseScore, visually and sonically distinct from house, techno, and big-room. It gives the gallery a neon, 1980s-future lane without overlapping the club-music candidates.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- Native Instruments, "What is synthwave / How to make a synthwave track" (2023-07-21): unhurried ~100 BPM starting point; four-on-the-floor kick, snare 2+4, 16th hats with room around the snare; layered analog synths entering every 4-8 bars.
- Synthwave Pro, "Exploring Typical Synthwave Drum Patterns" (2023-09-08): four-on-the-floor, snare 2+4, closed hats on eighths, open/closed hat dynamics; TR-808/909/Linn character.
- eMastered, "How to Make Synthwave" (2024-09-03): 80-140 BPM range; example 86 BPM ballad vs uptempo; analog bass eighths on chord roots.
- Sample Focus tempo guide: synthwave 80-118 BPM typical center.

## Instrumentation

| Part        | MusicXML / GM                      | Role                                 |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Synth Lead  | GM 82 saw                          | Bright analog topline.               |
| Synth Brass | GM 63                              | 80s stab chords on the chorus.       |
| Synth Bass  | GM 39                              | Eighth-note root sequence.           |
| Synth Pad   | GM 90 warm                         | Atmosphere from bar 1.               |
| Drum Kit    | kick 36, snare 38, hat 42, open 46 | Analog four-on-the-floor, 16th hats. |

## Tempo, key, length

- 108 BPM, 4/4, C minor (three flats). Driving but not house-fast.
- 72 bars = 160.0s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: Cm | Bb | Ab | G (i - VII - VI - V). Distinct from other minor loops in this set.

Kick every quarter. Snare 2+4. Hats 16ths with gaps on the snare attacks.

## Arrangement arc (72 bars)

1. Bars 1-8: pad + analog kit, no lead.
2. Bars 9-24: bass + brass stabs.
3. Bars 25-40: lead hook (verse analog).
4. Bars 41-48: breakdown, pad + tom-less hats.
5. Bars 49-64: chorus / peak (lead + brass).
6. Bars 65-72: outro, cadence on C.

## Anti-patterns

- Peak-time techno rumble (lane 07).
- Dutch squeaks (lane 03).
- Acoustic guitars.
- Copying any 80s film-score hook.
