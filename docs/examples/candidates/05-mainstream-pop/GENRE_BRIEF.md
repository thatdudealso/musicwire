# Mainstream pop production brief

Contemporary chart-pop shape and instrumentation. Original instrumental only. Distinct from British pop (lane 06) and alt-pop (lane 04).

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- BeatKey, "How to make pop music": chart pop clusters 120-128 BPM for dance-pop, but midtempo radio-pop often sits lower; kick 1+3 in verses, four-on-the-floor in choruses; snare/clap 2+4 always; intro 4-8 bars teases the hook; prechorus is the slingshot; chorus is the peak with layered lead.
- Wikipedia, "Pop music" (citing Warner): emphasis on recording/production, dance-oriented rhythms, verse-chorus design.
- MTO 30.2 Geary: post-millennial pop cycles of verse, prechorus, chorus, with drum-pattern change marking arrival.

This lane is US radio-pop: piano + synths + punchy kit, not guitar-anthem (lane 01) and not 2-step (lane 06).

## Tempo, key, length

- 104 BPM, 4/4, F major (one flat). Midtempo chart-pop, not dance-pop 124.
- 72 bars = 166.2s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: F | Am | Bb | C (I - iii - IV - V). Avoids I-V-vi-IV and I-IV-vi-V (used in other lanes).

Verse: kick 1+3, snare 2+4, hats on quarters.
Chorus: kick every quarter, hats eighths, crash on 1.

## Arrangement arc (72 bars)

1. Bars 1-8: intro hook tease (lead + piano, hats only).
2. Bars 9-24: verse.
3. Bars 25-32: prechorus lift.
4. Bars 33-48: chorus.
5. Bars 49-56: verse two.
6. Bars 57-64: chorus two.
7. Bars 65-72: postchorus tag on F.

## Anti-patterns

- Guitar-led pop-rock (lane 01).
- 2-step skipped kicks (lane 06).
- Piano-only ballad with no kit contrast.
- Copying chart-pop catalog hooks.
