# Metalcore production brief

Breakdowns, aggressive riffing, melodic contrast. Original instrumental only. Longer than the existing 32-bar gym-pump demo (`Forge Current` in D minor).

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- Songbrain, "How to Write Metalcore Breakdowns That Hit" (2026-04-20): keep tempo; cut subdivision in half for the breakdown; dead-air gap before the drop; one chug pattern per section; kick locked to chugs.
- Lyric Assistant, "How to Write Metalcore Songs" (2024-09-30): 140-190 BPM; breakdowns drop busy high end; use silence as weight.
- Orphiq metal map: metalcore 120-160 BPM, drop tunings, breakdowns, scream/clean contrast - here the "clean" is a melodic overdriven hook, not a vocal.
- MTO 27.1 Garza: breakdown riff type = slow time-feel, low-register unison, crash/china, staccato gaps.

Distinct from lane 08: this track **must** have a silence gap and a half-time breakdown. Distinct from the D-minor demo: new key (B minor), longer two-breakdown arc.

## Tempo, key, length

- 152 BPM, 4/4, B minor (two sharps). Drop-D-class B power chords without copying the D-minor demo.
- 104 bars = 163.2s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: Bm | G | D | A (i - VI - III - VII). One chug pattern per section.

## Arrangement arc (104 bars)

1. Bars 1-8: intro muted chugs + kick.
2. Bars 9-24: verse.
3. Bars 25-40: chorus (open + lead hook).
4. Bars 41-42: dead air (rest, then one hit).
5. Bars 43-58: breakdown 1 (quarter chugs, snare on 3).
6. Bars 59-74: chorus two.
7. Bars 75-76: second gap.
8. Bars 77-96: breakdown 2 (peak).
9. Bars 97-104: cadence on B.

## Anti-patterns

- Traditional gallop with no breakdown (lane 08).
- Slowing the click for the breakdown.
- Copying the D-minor gym-pump demo riff.
- Piano or clean-guitar substituting for distortion/kit.
- Mixing several chug patterns inside one section.
