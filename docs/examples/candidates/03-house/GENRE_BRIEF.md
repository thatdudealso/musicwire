# House production brief

Internal lane: electro / Dutch-house groove, syncopated leads, club energy (Afrojack energy class). Original instrumental only. Distinct from big-room festival EDM and from the existing C-minor showcase `Lantern Call`.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-01):

- Melodigging, "Dutch House": percussive, minimalist electro-house; tight tribal-leaning drums; syncopated rhythms; high-pitched bleepy/squeaky lead synths trading space with sub-heavy bass stabs; 126-130 BPM.
- Wikiwand / electro house: Dutch house pioneered late-2000s Netherlands; complex Latin-influenced drum kits, lower emphasis on long basslines, squeaky high-pitched leads.
- BeatKey, "How to make a house beat": 126 BPM is the most common house tempo; four-on-the-floor kick; clap/snare on 2 and 4; hats on eighths or 16ths; electro house 128-135 BPM.
- MusicRadar, "How to program 6 different four-to-the-floor grooves": house = kick every beat, clap 2+4, open hat on the off-beat between kicks.

## Tempo, key, length

- 128 BPM, 4/4, A minor (no sharps/flats). Distinct from C-minor showcase and G-minor big-room candidate.
- 88 bars = 165.0s.
- Divisions=4.

## Harmony and groove

Loop: Am | F | G | E (i - VI - VII - V). Club-safe minor with a V turn.

Kick every quarter. Snare 2+4. Closed hats eighths. Open hat on the offbeat after 2 and 4. Lead syncopates against the floor.

## Arrangement arc (88 bars)

1. Bars 1-8: kick + open-hat offbeats only.
2. Bars 9-24: groove + bass stabs; lead still tacet.
3. Bars 25-40: lead stabs enter, 8-bar call then 8-bar busier answer.
4. Bars 41-48: filter-style break (pad, no kick).
5. Bars 49-72: drop / main. Full kit, bass, squeaky hook.
6. Bars 73-80: percussion breakdown (kick remains).
7. Bars 81-88: hook reprise, cadence on A.

## Anti-patterns

- Big-room supersaw occupying the whole bar (that is lane 02).
- Piano house with acoustic kit.
- Copying the showcase `Lantern Call` C-minor climb.
- Four-bar loop with no percussion variation.
- Pitched drum notes.
