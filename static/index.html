01 - product demo

Pier Light - brief to MP3

For humans

An agent writes a 16-bar piano piece, pays to check it, pays to render it, and plays the MP3. That is the whole Musicwire loop in one take.

For agents

solo piano · style=piano · key=C major · tempo=96 · 16 bars 4/4
validate $0.10 then render formats:["mp3"] (solo $0.25)
poll until qc passed · GET the MP3

02 - festival EDM

Skyline Surge

For humans

A festival anthem: pads open the room, four-on-the-floor arrives, a snare roll builds, then the drop hits.

For agents

ensemble · style=festival-EDM · key=F minor · tempo=128 · 24 bars
parts: GM82 lead, GM39 bass, GM89 pad, unpitched house kit
render formats:["mp3","midi"] (ensemble $0.50)

04 - modern country

Cedar Mile

For humans

A driving Nashville cut: acoustic backbone, Tele-class lead, punchy backbeat. No 808s, no trap hats.

For agents

parts: GM26 steel acoustic, GM30 Tele-class lead, GM34 finger bass, unpitched kit
kick 1+3 / snare 2+4 · no 808 · ensemble $0.50

[ FOR AGENTS AND HUMANS ]

The useful path is short.

An agent supplies the score and follows the response. A human opens the MP3 in a browser or audio player, imports MIDI into a music app, and keeps the receipt with the work.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Use the MCP server or a plain HTTPS request.

  2. 2

    Request

    Submit MusicXML and ask for MP3 and MIDI.

  3. 3

    Receive

    Poll the job, open the audio link, and save what matters.
